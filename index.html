🛡️ Safety Guardrails

MediGuide AI only explains information contained in the uploaded medical document.

It does not create a new diagnosis, prescribe medicines, change medicine doses, or replace a healthcare professional.

If information is not present in the uploaded document, MediGuide AI should clearly say that the information was not found.

⚠️ Important Safety Notice

MediGuide AI is an educational medical-document understanding tool.

Always discuss your medical report, medicines and treatment decisions with a qualified healthcare professional.

For severe or emergency symptoms, seek immediate medical care.
